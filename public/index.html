About Us

Botswana signage and branding since 2009.

Established in 2009, Sign Base is a trusted signage and branding company in Botswana. We specialise in designing, manufacturing and installing high-quality signage, corporate fascia, vehicle branding, stickers, printed apparel and number plates.

With over 17 years of experience, we are committed to delivering innovative designs, exceptional quality and reliable service, helping businesses of all sizes stand out and make a lasting impression.

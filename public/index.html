About Sign Base

A Botswana signage manufacturer built on workmanship you can see from the road.

Sign Base is a Botswana-based signage manufacturing and printing company. We make the signs, stickers, printed garments and number plates that businesses rely on to be found and trusted.

Our mission: deliver durable, well-finished branding on time, whether the order is one sticker or a nationwide rollout.

We serve businesses of all sizes: corporate clients, dealerships, small shops and individual customers.

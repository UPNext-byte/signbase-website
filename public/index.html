About Us

Established in 2009, Sign Base is a trusted signage and branding company in Botswana. We specialise in designing, manufacturing and installing high-quality signage, corporate fascia, vehicle branding, stickers, printed apparel and number plates. With over 17 years of experience, we help businesses of all sizes stand out through quality workmanship, creative solutions and reliable service.
